# Deliverables and media contract

## Deliverable inventory

| ID | Exact output | Audience and purpose | Behavioral gate | Format and QA | Supporting evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DELIV-1 | To define | To define | To define | To define | To define |

## Provenance

For each output freeze source, scene, assets, controller or policy, checkpoint,
configuration, seed or scenario, raw run, telemetry, render or export command, and
checksum. Separate source evidence, candidate, and certified master locations.

## Media, when applicable

Specify clean render versus viewport evidence, camera views, overlays, synchronization,
resolution, aspect ratio, frame rate, codec, pixel format, duration, audio, and naming.
Preserve uninterrupted source runs and rejected candidates.

## Technical QA

Verify metadata, expected frame or sample count, full decode or load, beginning,
middle, and end, duration, dimensions, synchronization, checksum, local usability, and
backup parity.

## Visual or behavioral QA

Inspect the behavior the deliverable claims, camera correctness, labels, unintended UI,
failures, edits, and consistency with telemetry. Label scripted, baseline, trained,
replay, simulation, and physical status truthfully.

## Promotion

Promote only a uniquely named, acceptance-passing master. Record rejection reasons,
promotion date, acceptance rows, final hash, backup location, and claims enabled.
